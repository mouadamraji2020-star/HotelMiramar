Welcome

Your home by the sea in Tangier

Hôtel Miramar sits right on the Corniche, across the road from Tangier Beach and a short walk from Tanja Marina Bay. Many of our rooms look straight out over the bay towards Spain.

Our team at reception is here day and night to help with anything — from your arrival by ferry or plane to finding the best way through the Medina.
